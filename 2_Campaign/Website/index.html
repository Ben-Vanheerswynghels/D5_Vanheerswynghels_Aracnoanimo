THE ARACHNOANIMO IDEA

From looking at a spider to feeling more comfortable around one.

ONE STEP AT A TIME ✳
01 WHY ARACHNOANIMO?

Fear should not have to control everyday life.

For some young people, unexpectedly seeing a spider at home can cause panic, avoidance and stress. Arachnoanimo offers a friendlier way to approach that fear.

This is a welcoming, playful festival where you can explore spiders gradually, choose your own pace and learn without feeling judged.

02 THE EXPERIENCE

Your pace.
Your choice.

There is no need to rush. Arachnoanimo is built around three simple ideas that make every step feel more manageable.

01

Control

Choose how close you get. You decide what you are comfortable with and when you are ready for the next step.

02

Small steps

Start with easy encounters and build up gradually. There is no competition and no expected pace.

03

No judgement

Fear is taken seriously here. Come as you are, ask questions and explore without embarrassment.
